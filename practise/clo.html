How Foundling works

No forms to chase, no lost-property office to call twice. Three steps, start to finish.

01

Post it

Describe what you lost or found — category, location, and roughly when. A photo helps but isn't required.

02

Get matched

Foundling checks new reports against open ones and flags likely matches for both sides to review.

03

Reunite

Confirm details privately in-app, then arrange pickup at a campus safe-drop point.
